# Indicator Combinations by Regime

## Bull Trend (ADX > 25, price > SMA200)
**Primary:** EMA(12/26) crossover
**Secondary:** MACD histogram direction
**Volume:** OBV confirming new highs
**Avoid:** RSI overbought signals (can stay overbought for weeks)

## Bear Trend (ADX > 25, price < SMA200)
**Primary:** EMA(12/26) bearish crossover
**Secondary:** ADX rising
**Volume:** OBV making new lows
**Avoid:** RSI oversold signals (can stay oversold for weeks)

## Ranging Market (ADX < 20)
**Primary:** RSI(14) mean reversion
**Secondary:** Bollinger Bands(20,2)
**Volume:** Decreasing volume at extremes
**Avoid:** All moving average crossovers

## High Volatility (VIX > 25)
**Primary:** ATR(14) for position sizing only
**Secondary:** Bollinger Bands(20,2) for range boundaries
**Volume:** Spike confirmation on breakout attempts
**Avoid:** All momentum indicators (whipsaws)
